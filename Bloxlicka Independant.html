Left thumb walks, right thumb looks. Tap Run to toggle walking and running. Grab rocks, crystals, ducks, beach balls and mushrooms; hold to charge, release to throw. Stand next to a friend and press Grab to pull them around — they're stuck for 3 seconds before they can break free. A well-aimed throw knocks them over. Wade into a pond and look down to dive. Walk into caves and ruins to discover them — glowing ore nodes are embedded in the walls: stand beside one and tap Grab to mine it for orbs. Press Grab beside a landmark for a question to chat about; the arrow in the top-left points to the nearest undiscovered place.
